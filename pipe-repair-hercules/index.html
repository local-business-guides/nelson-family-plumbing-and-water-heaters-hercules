Pipe repair addresses a problem with a plumbing pipe, such as a leak or visible damage. What happens during a pipe repair visit

A pipe repair visit starts with understanding the symptoms and assessing the affected area. The plumber can explain what they find and outline the work they recommend. Depending on the location and condition of the pipe, the repair may require access to the area around it. Ask what needs to be moved or cleared before work begins, and whether the water supply needs to be shut off during the repair.

Before the visit, you can prepare a few details:

  • Point out where you first noticed moisture or a leak.
  • Note whether the problem is constant or comes and goes.
  • Mention any changes you’ve seen since it started.
  • Keep the area accessible if you can do so safely.
  • Write down questions about the repair, timing, and pricing.

After the work, ask what was repaired and whether you should watch for anything in particular. Avoid covering a wet area or making a quick patch your only response when the source is unclear. If water is actively leaking or the situation is getting worse, call a professional and explain what’s happening.
